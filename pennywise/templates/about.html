About Pennywise Cosmetics

From humble beginnings to Trinidad & Tobago's largest health and beauty retail chain — our story is one of passion, perseverance, and an unwavering commitment to making beauty accessible to all.

Our Story

The late Mr Lall Paladee began his journey to success from a very humble home. He would walk door to door selling pins and clips daily while his wife would help by earning some money as a seamstress.

He won a bicycle in a raffle which he used to ride for many miles daily, still selling buttons and other haberdasheries door to door. Through hard work and dedication, he built what is now Trinidad & Tobago's premier health and beauty destination.

Today, Pennywise Cosmetics operates multiple locations across Trinidad & Tobago, serving thousands of customers daily with the widest range of cosmetics, health products, and beauty supplies at the most competitive prices.
